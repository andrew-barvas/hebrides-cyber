# Marketing Plan — First 6 Months

## Objectives
- Acquire first 20 paying customers (mix local + remote).
- Build a pipeline for recurring revenue via Security Score subscriptions.
- Establish Hebrides Cyber as the trusted local security partner.

## Channels & Tactics

### Local outreach (weeks 1–8)
- Email + phone outreach to local businesses, churches, tourism operators, and charities. Offer a free Security Score scan.
- Attend local business groups and chambers; offer a free security talk.
- Partner with local web agencies for referrals.

### Content & SEO (ongoing)
- Publish 2 blog posts per month, e.g. "How to secure a Next.js app", "Top 10 security mistakes for small businesses".
- Create local landing pages: "Security for Hebrides tourism sites", "Security for churches in Lewis".
- Use the free Security Score one-page report as a shareable, SEO-friendly lead magnet.

### Developer community & remote leads
- Post case studies on Hacker News, Indie Hackers, and relevant subreddits.
- Share technical before/after threads on X/Twitter and LinkedIn.
- Offer a discounted first audit on Upwork and respond to security RFPs.

### Paid & partnerships
- Small Google Ads budget testing "web security audit small business UK" (£200/mo trial).
- Partner with hosting providers and local ISPs for referrals.

### Retention
- Monthly newsletter with security tips and client highlights.
- Quarterly webinar: "Security for small teams", with live Q&A.

## KPIs (first 6 months)
- Leads per month: 30
- Conversion rate (lead → paid audit): 10–15%
- Monthly recurring revenue by month 6: £1,200–£2,500
