## 3. Local Benefit — Digital Resilience for Western Isles Businesses

### The problem

Western Isles businesses are increasingly dependent on websites — tourism
bookings, online retail, customer communication — but have almost no
access to affordable cybersecurity expertise. The nearest CREST-certified
security consultancy is in Inverness. A basic manual security assessment
costs £300-500, putting it out of reach for most small businesses.

Meanwhile, cyber threats don't respect geography. A church website in
Point is as vulnerable to automated attacks as a business in Glasgow.

### What Hebrides Cyber offers locally

1. **Free security scans for 50 local businesses in Year 1** — any
   Western Isles business with a website gets a free comprehensive scan
   with a plain-English report and specific fix instructions
2. **25% discount for all Western Isles businesses** on ongoing
   subscription plans (£15/month instead of £20 for Basic)
3. **2-3 free cyber hygiene workshops per year** at local venues
   (An Lanntair, library, Business Gateway premises)
4. **Cyber Essentials preparation support** — the compliance framework
   increasingly required for public-sector contracts, made accessible
   to local trades and businesses bidding on council work

### Employment

- First hire within 12 months (contractor pen tester)
- Medium-term: support engineer and marketing assistant — both roles
  suitable for island-based remote work
- 80%+ of revenue comes from outside the islands, bringing money into
  the local economy
