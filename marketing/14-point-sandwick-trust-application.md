# Point and Sandwick Trust — Community Benefit Application

**Draft for owner review. The Point and Sandwick Trust awards community
benefit funding from Beinn Ghrideag wind farm revenue to projects
benefiting the Point and Sandwick area and wider Lewis community. Verify
current application windows, criteria, and formats directly with the Trust
before submission.**

---

## 1. Summary

Hebrides Cyber is a Stornoway-based cybersecurity startup offering free
website security scanning to Lewis community organisations and local
businesses. This application requests support for the community-benefit
component of the business — specifically, the free scan programme and
cyber hygiene workshops for local organisations.

---

## 2. Community Benefit Proposal

### Free security scanning for Lewis community organisations

Many Lewis community organisations — churches, charities, community
councils, sports clubs, volunteer groups — have websites managed by
volunteers with no security expertise. These sites handle donations,
event bookings, and community information but have never been checked
for security vulnerabilities.

Hebrides Cyber will provide:

- **Free annual security scans for all Lewis community organisations**
  that request one — churches, charities, community councils, sports
  clubs, and similar bodies. No limit on numbers for genuine community
  organisations.
- **A plain-English security report** with each scan — not technical
  jargon, but specific, actionable instructions a volunteer website
  manager can follow ("change this setting," "update this plugin,"
  "ask your hosting provider to fix this").
- **Follow-up support** — if a volunteer can't act on the report alone,
  Hebrides Cyber will provide guidance on next steps at no charge.

### Cyber hygiene workshops

2-3 free community workshops per year, targeted at:
- **Church and charity website managers** — "Keeping your organisation's
  website safe: a practical guide"
- **Local small businesses** — "Cyber Essentials: what it means for your
  business and how to prepare"
- **Community groups and volunteers** — "Protecting personal data online:
  what every volunteer should know"

Venues: community halls in Point and Sandwick, An Lanntair in Stornoway,
or online via video call for those who can't travel.

## 4. About Hebrides Cyber

The founder lives in Stornoway and has 15+ years of cybersecurity
experience at Lockheed Martin and Leidos, plus Scottish public-sector
digital transformation delivery. The product is live, with 914 automated
tests and 20 enterprise security features — the same technology that
large companies pay thousands for, offered free to local community
organisations.

This isn't a mainland company claiming island connections — it's a
genuine Lewis-based business giving back to the local community.
