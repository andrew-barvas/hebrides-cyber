# Social Media & Local Press Playbook

Companion to `marketing/06-marketing-plan.md` (channel priorities/KPIs) and
`marketing/02-branding.md` (voice/tone — "professional, direct, technical
but accessible; locality used as trust and story, not limitation"). This
doc owns platform-specific setup and content strategy; it doesn't restate
the channel-priority reasoning already in 06.

**What this doc does not do**: create the actual accounts. That's a
"register with your own identity, email, and phone verification" action —
yours to do, not something delegated here. Everything below is what you
paste in once you've created each account, plus the content strategy to
run afterward.

## 1. Platform priority — and why, given this specific product

Ranked by actual buyer presence, not generic "every startup needs socials"
advice:

| Priority | Platform | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **LinkedIn** | The SME/agency buyer and the Cyber Essentials/compliance-triggered buyer (this product's actual positioning, per `docs/COMPETITIVE-ANALYSIS.md` §9) are professionally active here. Also the single best place to surface the founder's own Lockheed Martin/Leidos/Scottish public-sector background — a genuine, real differentiator (see `10-formalisation-and-funding-roadmap.md`'s revised §6(c)) that a generic company-page post would waste. |
| 2 | **Facebook** | Lower relevance for the compliance/developer buyer, but this is genuinely where local Outer Hebrides business owners, tourism operators, and community groups actually are — Facebook groups for local business/tourism are real and active in a way LinkedIn isn't for this specific segment. Matches `06-marketing-plan.md`'s local-outreach channel directly. |
| 3 | **X/Twitter** | Lowest priority of the three, but the right home for the developer/agency technical-content channel `06-marketing-plan.md` §3 already scoped (remediation-PR showcases, before/after threads) — a different audience from LinkedIn's buyer-side content. |

Not recommending Instagram/TikTok — no buyer or press audience for this
product is there, and spreading a solo operator's limited posting capacity
across a 4th platform with no clear audience is the mistake to avoid, not
a gap to fill.

## 4. Content pillars

Four repeating pillars, so posting doesn't default to "whatever comes to
mind" — grounded in what's actually real about the product (per this
session's own "docs describe reality" standing rule extended to marketing
content): never claim a capability or result that isn't true yet.

1. **Proof-of-work** — a real remediation PR, a real New/Fixed diff, a real
   Cyber Essentials mapping output. **Blocked on real customer consent**
   (`06-marketing-plan.md` already flags this) — until then, use
   sanitised/synthetic examples clearly labelled as such, never implied to
   be a real customer's data.
2. **Founder credibility** — the Lockheed Martin/Leidos/Scottish
   public-sector background, framed as "why this exists" content, not a
   CV post. LinkedIn-primary.
3. **Local security education** — practical, non-salesy tips for Outer
   Hebrides tourism operators, churches, and local trades (matches the
   dedicated landing pages already live at `/tourism`, `/churches`,
   `/local-trades`). Facebook-primary.
4. **Technical/developer content** — CI-gate-on-new-findings-only,
   evidence-discipline scanning philosophy, the remediation ladder — the
   genuinely differentiated technical angles from
   `docs/COMPETITIVE-ANALYSIS.md` §5. X/Twitter and LinkedIn.

## 6. Local press & company outreach

### Target outlets (verify current editorial contacts directly before
pitching — none confirmed live here)

- **Stornoway Gazette** — the direct local paper; a Stornoway-founded
  cyber security startup with a Lockheed Martin/Leidos-background founder
  is a genuinely strong local-interest story, not a stretch pitch.
- **West Highland Free Press** — wider Highlands & Islands readership,
  same local-founder-story angle.
- **The Press and Journal** — larger North of Scotland regional paper;
  worth a pitch once there's a concrete hook (funding secured, first
  milestone, a HIE grant if `10-formalisation-and-funding-roadmap.md`'s
  case succeeds).
- **Insider.co.uk / Scotland's tech press** — for a "digital economy
  diversification in the islands" angle specifically, which ties directly
  into the HIE funding case's own community-benefit argument.
- **BBC Scotland / BBC Alba** — a longer-shot pitch, worth trying once
  there's a stronger hook (funding, a notable local client, a milestone)
  rather than at pure launch.

### Press-release angle (draft — adapt once real milestones exist)

> **Stornoway-founded cyber security startup brings affordable protection
> to Outer Hebrides businesses**
>
> Hebrides Cyber, founded in Stornoway by [founder name], a security and
> cloud delivery specialist with 15+ years' experience including work with
> major US defence contractors Lockheed Martin and Leidos, is bringing
> automated, developer-grade security scanning to small businesses across
> the Outer Hebrides and beyond. The service maps directly to the UK's
> Cyber Essentials scheme, giving local tourism operators, churches, and
> trades an affordable route to a compliance standard increasingly
> required by insurers, banks, and larger clients — at a fraction of the
> cost of a traditional consultancy engagement.

Don't send this as-is — it needs a real, current hook (a launch date, a
funding milestone, a client count) to be newsworthy rather than generic;
treat it as a structural template, not a finished release.
